Our story

A field of stillness

Northfield is named after a place you might know too: a field at the edge of the day, where everything goes quiet. The wind in the grass. Moss under your hand. Nowhere to be.

Most days don't leave room for a trip like that. So we brought the field to your kitchen. Simple herbs, gently blended, made for the small pause between one thing and the next.

No big promises. Just a warm cup and a few minutes that belong to you.

Your daily pause, in a cup.
The rest ritual

Brew. Breathe. Taste.

A rest ritual doesn't need an hour or an app. It needs three minutes and a kettle. This is how every Northfield cup begins.

01

Brew

Put the kettle on. Leave your phone in the other room.

02

Breathe

Three minutes of steeping. Three minutes of slow breathing.

03

Taste

Take the first sip in silence and notice the warmth.
